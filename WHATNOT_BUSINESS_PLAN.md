## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We buy **pre-packaged perfume sample packs** in bulk at volume discounts and sell them via Whatnot live shows. No decanting. No equipment. No gray areas. Buy sealed → sell sealed.

**Why This Works:**
- Buy at $1.50-$5/unit in bulk, sell for $5-$15/unit live = 3-5x markup
- Carded designer vials retail $4-$8+ each on resale markets
- Discovery sets / mystery bundles = perfect live auction format
- Lightweight shipping (<$4/package), nearly zero breakage
- Fragrance community is obsessive about sampling — insane repeat purchase rate
- Year-round demand, spikes at holidays/Valentine's/Mother's Day

## SHIPPING — KEEP IT CHEAP

### USPS Rules for Perfume
- Perfume IS mailable USPS Ground Advantage (NOT air)
- Max 16 oz per package
- Seal in secondary packaging (ziplock bag)
- Absorbent material in packing
- Mark "Surface/Ground Only" or ORM-D

### Packing Process (Person 2)
1. Vial/bottle in bubble wrap
2. Into small ziplock bag (secondary containment)
3. Into padded mailer or small box
4. Tissue or paper as absorbent
5. Label via Whatnot integrated USPS shipping

### Costs
- Padded mailer: $0.20-$0.40 (bulk from Amazon/Uline)
- USPS First Class (under 4oz): $3.50-$4.50
- Charge buyer $3-$5 flat ship on Whatnot → nearly break even on shipping
